**British Columbia** is the West Coast region in the official guide. It joined Confederation in **1871** on the promise of a transcontinental railway.

## Places

- Capital: **Victoria**, on Vancouver Island
- Largest city: **Vancouver**, a major Pacific port
- The **Rocky Mountains** and Coast Mountains dominate travel and climate
- A milder, wetter coast; a drier interior

## Economy and people

Forestry, mining, film and television, tourism, and Pacific trade all matter. The port of Vancouver is one of the busiest in North America. Immigration from Asia has shaped Greater Vancouver for more than a century, including Chinese, South Asian, Japanese, and later many other communities—alongside First Nations whose territories cover the whole province.

Treaties were not signed across most of B.C. in the nineteenth century the way they were on the Prairies. Modern treaty and title cases are part of public life here.

## Test traps

- Victoria is the capital; Vancouver is the largest city
- Yukon is not part of the West Coast region (it is Northern)
