# Prepare Citizenship

Free study site for the Canadian citizenship test: https://preparecitizenship.ca/

## Summary

Original chapter lessons, a large practice question bank, flashcards, and timed mock exams (20 questions, 45 minutes, 15/20 to pass). English and French UI. No account, no ads. Progress stays on the device; the site works offline after the first visit.

**Not affiliated with Immigration, Refugees and Citizenship Canada (IRCC).** Questions are original paraphrases for practice, not the real exam bank. Always study with the official guide:

- https://www.canada.ca/en/immigration-refugees-citizenship/services/canadian-citizenship/test/study.html
- https://www.canada.ca/content/dam/ircc/migration/ircc/english/pdf/pub/discover.pdf

## Privacy and auth

No login and no OAuth. There is no protected API. Study data never leaves the browser unless the user exports it. See OAuth protected-resource metadata at `/.well-known/oauth-protected-resource` (public resource, empty authorization servers).
